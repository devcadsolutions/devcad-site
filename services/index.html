How a project runs

Fully remote, with working builds early so nothing is a surprise at the end.

  1. Send the workflow

    Describe the task, the software and versions you run, and where it goes wrong. A screen recording helps more than a spec.

  2. Agree the scope

    A short call or a few messages. You get a recommendation: a script, an add-in, an APS workflow, or sometimes just a better process.

  3. Build on your files

    I build and test against your real models and drawings, and send working versions as I go so your team can try them early.

  4. Roll it out

    Installer, a short guide and a handover your team can follow without me in the room.

Not sure which of these you need?
