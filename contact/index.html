What to send

Rough notes are fine. These five points cover most of what I need to give you a useful answer.

  • Software involved

    Revit, AutoCAD, APS / ACC, Excel or Sheets, and the versions you run.

  • The manual steps today

    What someone actually clicks, copies, or checks, and how often.

  • Where the process breaks

    The step that causes rework, delays, or depends on one person remembering.

  • What a better handoff looks like

    The output you want: a clean sheet set, an export, a report, a checked model.

  • Rough timeline

    Any deadline, project milestone, or simply “whenever it’s ready.”

Want a head start? Copy a plain-text brief and paste it into your email.
